Hey, I'm Kumar

Learning how AI works by building with it.

I'm Kumar, an AI engineer and researcher interested in building language models and understanding what makes them work. I'm a final-year engineering student, AWS Student Builder Center Campus Lead, and former ML research intern at IISc Bengaluru.

I work across the LLM lifecycle, from data curation and tokenization to continued pre-training, fine-tuning, and evaluation. I'm especially interested in low-resource Indic languages and world models.

I build with Python, PyTorch, and the Hugging Face ecosystem, combining hands-on engineering with systematic experimentation to turn research questions into working systems.

I like learning from first principles. My motto is: learn, build, lead. I also make art, and I tend to see patterns in everything.
